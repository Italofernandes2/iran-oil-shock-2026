# Iran Oil Shock 2026 — Brent & Gas Price Analysis

Analysis of the largest oil supply shock in modern history,
triggered by the Iran-Israel War that began on February 28, 2026.

## 📊 About the Project

This project analyzes how Brent crude, WTI, Dubai crude,
and US gas prices behaved during the first 21 days of the
Iran-Israel War, including the closure of the Strait of Hormuz.

## 🗂️ Datasets

- **iran_war_oil_prices_daily_2026.csv** — Daily oil and gas prices
- **iran_war_gas_prices_by_state.csv** — Gas prices by US state
- **iran_war_key_events_timeline.csv** — Key war events timeline

## 🔍 Project Structure

1. Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
2. Data Cleaning & Preprocessing
3. Feature Engineering
4. Data Visualization & Insights

## 💡 Key Insights

- Brent crude rose from $73 to $110 in just 21 days (+50.9%)
- Strait of Hormuz traffic collapsed from 138 to 4 ships/day (97% drop)
- Week 1 (Mar 1–4) was the most volatile period of the shock
- Kentucky was the most impacted US state with +31% fuel price increase
